# Voice-over scripts

One script per video. Each is about 60–90 seconds at a relaxed pace, and the lines follow the on-screen captions in order.

---

## Client guide

### client-01: Logging in & your dashboard
> Welcome! In this short video we'll log in and take a quick look around.
> Open the portal link your account manager sent you. Enter your username or email, then your password, and click Sign In.
> The first time you log in, you'll be asked to choose your own password.
> This is your dashboard, a quick summary of all your tickets.
> On the left you'll find Tickets, where you raise and track issues; Messages, to chat with your project team; and Reviews, to tell us how we're doing.
> Next, let's raise your first ticket.

### client-02: Raising a ticket
> To report a problem or request a change, open Tickets and click Create Ticket.
> Start with a short, clear subject. Then choose the project it's about.
> Pick a type: Issue for something that's broken, Request for new work.
> Set the priority so the team knows how urgent it is.
> In the description, explain the steps you took, what you expected and what actually happened. Screenshots help a lot, so attach them here.
> Click Create Ticket, and that's it. Your ticket is on the board and the team has been notified.

### client-03: Tracking a ticket & replying
> Your tickets live on the board, and each column is a stage: Assigned, In Process, Resolved and so on.
> Click a ticket to open it. The status tells you exactly where things stand. This one is In Process.
> Updates from the team show up in the conversation below.
> To reply, type your message and press Enter.
> You'll get a notification whenever your ticket is updated or resolved.

### client-04: Chatting with your team
> For quick questions, use Messages.
> Your conversations are listed on the left. Open one to read and reply in real time.
> You can add emojis and attach files too.
> For anything that needs to be tracked and fixed, raise a ticket instead. That way nothing gets lost.

### client-05: Leaving a review
> We'd love your feedback. Open Reviews and click Create Review.
> Give it a title, write a few words about your experience, and click Create Review.
> Your review is shared with the team, and you can edit or delete it later.
> That's everything. Thanks for working with us!

## Employee guide

### employee-01: Getting started
> Welcome aboard! Let's log in and look around.
> Use the username and password HR gave you. On your first login you'll be asked to change your password.
> Your dashboard shows your tickets, hours and team announcements.
> In the sidebar: Tickets is your work board. Requests tracks things you've asked for. Messages is for chatting with teammates and clients.
> Teams and Projects show who you work with and what you work on. Timesheet is where you log your hours and leave.
> Interviews lists any interview panels you're on, and Shoutouts is where we celebrate each other.

### employee-02: Working on your tickets
> Your board shows every ticket assigned to you, grouped by status.
> Here's a new one. Open it and read the client's description.
> When you start, change the status to In Process and click Update Ticket.
> Keep the client informed with comments. Public comments are visible to the client, so the app asks you to confirm.
> For notes only your team should see, use the Internal tab.
> When the work is done, move it to Resolved so QA or the client can verify it.

### employee-03: Filling in your timesheet
> Log your hours every working day.
> The calendar shows each day's status: pending, approved or rejected. Select today.
> Choose the day type: office, client site or work from home. The same list has Weekend, Public Holiday, and Half or Full Day Leave.
> Add a task: describe what you did, pick the project, link the ticket if there is one, and enter the hours.
> Worked on several things? Click Add Another Task.
> Click Save Timesheet. Your manager and HR will review it.
> Leave days are deducted from your balance only after HR approves them.

### employee-04: Teams, projects & requests
> Teams shows your team, its lead and its members.
> Projects lists every project you're part of. Open one to see its tickets and team.
> Requests shows the status of anything you've asked HR or admin for.
> And if you're on an interview panel, your interviews appear here.

### employee-05: Messages & shoutouts
> Messages lets you chat with clients and teammates in real time.
> Open a conversation and reply. Use New Group Chat to talk to a whole project team.
> Shoutouts let you thank a colleague in front of the whole company. Click Create Shoutout, add a title and a message, and post it.
> That's it. Welcome to the team!
